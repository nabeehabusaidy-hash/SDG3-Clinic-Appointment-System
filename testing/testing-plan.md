Testing Objective: The objective of testing the Clinic appointment system prototype is to evaluate the usability and functionality of the system . It focuses on making sure the users can successfully and easily perform the key tasks such as viewing available appointments slots , booking appointments , managing existing appointments. Evaluating this will help identify any potential issues the system could face  and makes sure the prototype is meeting the basic user requirements and system requirements.
Testing Method : Usability Testing. Firstly , The users will be asked to perform common tasks such as viewing available appointments slots , booking appointments , managing existing appointments. Secondly, the tester will observe whether the tasks can be completed successfully and then the outcomes and feedbacks will be recorded. Finally, the data will be used for a final evaluation.
